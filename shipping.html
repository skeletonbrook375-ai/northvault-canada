Shipping Policy

NorthVaults ships trading card mystery boxes exclusively within Canada with free shipping and cash-on-delivery (COD) collection.

Shipping Cost & Coverage

  • Shipping Rate: Free ($0.00 CAD) on all orders with no minimum spend.
  • Service Area: All Canadian provinces and territories (ON, QC, BC, AB, MB, SK, NS, NB, NL, PE, YT, NT, NU). We do not ship outside Canada at this time.

Processing & Delivery Timeframes

  • Order Processing: 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding Canadian statutory holidays).
  • Estimated Transit Time: 3–8 business days after dispatch, depending on destination province or territory.
  • Total Estimated Delivery: 4–11 business days from order placement.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

No online payment is collected at checkout. Once your order ships, you will receive an email confirmation with tracking details. The courier collects the full order total (item subtotal plus applicable provincial/territorial tax) in cash upon delivery.

Damaged or Missing Shipments

If your shipment arrives damaged or does not arrive within the expected delivery window, please contact us promptly via our Contact page with your order reference number.
