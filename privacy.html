Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information NorthVaults collects when you browse or place a Cash on Delivery (COD) order on northvaults.shop.

Information We Collect

When you place an order or submit a question, we collect only the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, and Canadian shipping address.

How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfil, pack, ship, and deliver your Cash on Delivery order across Canada.
  • To send order confirmation, tracking updates, and respond to support or return requests.

Third-Party Form Processing & Data Sharing

Order and contact form submissions are transmitted via FormSubmit to notify our fulfilment inbox. Your cart contents are stored locally in your browser's storage. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Contact & Data Requests

To request updates or deletion of your order contact information, please reach out via our Contact page.
