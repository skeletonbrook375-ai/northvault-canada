FOUR-POINT VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

How We Guarantee 100% Sealed Authenticity

In the vintage trading card market, resealed and tampered boxes are the greatest risk. NorthVault follows an uncompromising forensic protocol for every sealed display box prior to vaulting.

1

Factory Seam & Shrink Inspection

We inspect shrink wrap overlap patterns, vertical heat seal seams, shrink tension, and factory micro-perforations under 40x magnification to confirm original manufacturer seal integrity.

2

Precision Weight Variance (±0.2g)

Using laboratory-grade calibrated scales, the total box mass is matched against our historical database of verified untouched 36-pack displays to detect missing packs or foreign insertions.

3

Digital Endoscopy & Box Flap Alignment

Non-invasive micro-optical fiber cameras inspect side cardboard tab folds through micro-perforations to verify no inner cardboard flap has been opened or glued post-production.

4

Tamper-Evident Canadian Vault Casing

Once authenticated, each box is housed in custom 8mm acrylic with a serialized hologram security seal recorded on our internal ledger and supplied with a physical Certificate of Inspection.
