Returns & Refund Policy

We offer straightforward returns on unopened mystery boxes across Canada, plus the option to refuse an unopened package at delivery.

Refusing Delivery

Because orders ship Cash on Delivery (COD), you can refuse an unopened box when the courier arrives and pay nothing.

14-Day Returns (By Mail)

  • Return Window: Unopened boxes can be returned by mail within 14 days of delivery.
  • Condition Requirement: Returned boxes must be unopened and in their original outer packaging condition. Opened mystery boxes cannot be returned unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.
  • Return Shipping Cost: The customer pays return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or wrong.

Refund Method & Timing

Once we receive and inspect the returned box, your refund is issued by Interac e-Transfer within 5 business days of receiving the return.

How to Start a Return

Please send your order reference number and details through our Contact page before mailing your return so we can provide return instructions.
